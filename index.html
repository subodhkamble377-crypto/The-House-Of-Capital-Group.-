FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you start a conversation.

Can I start with a small SIP?

Yes. The suitable amount depends on your cash flow, goals and investment horizon. A SIP can be increased later as income changes.

Is a mutual fund return guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund investments are market-linked and returns are not guaranteed. Past performance does not assure future results.

What is financial planning?

It is a structured process of understanding your goals, resources, risks and time horizons and then organising your financial actions around them.

Should I choose SIP or lump sum?

It depends on your cash flow, available capital, goal and risk tolerance. Both are investment methods rather than inherently suitable choices for everyone.

What information is useful for a first review?

Approximate income, monthly expenses, existing investments, loans/liabilities, insurance cover and major future goals are useful starting points.
